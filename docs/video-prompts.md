# Video generation prompts for the 4 sample clips

Prompts for a text-to-video tool (Veo 3, Sora 2, Kling, Runway or similar) to make the 4 AI-generated sample clips. Each clip is a short story built from 8 to 10 shots of about 8 seconds, joined into one file. Each clip mixes calm home scenes, where products fit, with one or two sensitive moments, where Pausal must hold the ad back. Together the 4 clips cover all six safety categories: violence, death or grief, sexual, medical trauma, child focused and intense dialogue.

## Rules for every shot

- Paste the clip's **style line** and **cast** at the start of every shot prompt, unchanged, so faces, rooms and light stay the same across shots. If the tool supports a reference image or "character" feature, generate the cast once and reuse it.
- Each shot holds one setting and one action. A cut between shots is a new scene for the pipeline, so do not ask for two places in one shot.
- Show ordinary, unbranded home goods clearly: pans, kettles, mugs, lamps, sofas, rugs, bedding, shoes, bags, backpacks, desk chairs. These are what the catalog sells.
- Always add: "No logos, no readable brand names, no on-screen text, no subtitles, no watermark."
- Sensitive shots stay mild: no blood beyond a small cut, no gore, no nudity. They only need to be clear enough for a judge to flag them.
- Audio: ambient room sound on every shot. Spoken lines only where a shot lists them (clip 2 is the dialogue clip).
- Target per clip: 60 to 120 seconds, 16:9, 1080p or 720p, under 25 MB after export.

## Clip 1: Sunday kitchen

**Style line:** Warm naturalistic short film, 16:9, soft morning daylight through a window, handheld but steady camera, shallow depth of field, muted warm colors, realistic people, a bright modern home kitchen with wooden counters and white tiles.

**Cast:** MAYA, a woman in her mid 30s with dark curly hair tied up, wearing a grey knit sweater. LEO, her 7-year-old son with short brown hair and a striped t-shirt.

1. Wide shot. Maya fills a matte white electric kettle at the sink and sets it on its base. A small radio plays quietly. Safe.
2. Close-up. Maya grinds coffee in a hand grinder and pours water from the kettle into a glass pour-over carafe; steam rises. Safe.
3. Medium shot. Maya cracks eggs into a black cast-iron skillet on the stove, then adds sliced tomatoes; sizzling sound. Safe.
4. Over-the-shoulder shot. Maya slices bread on a bamboo cutting board with a chef's knife, then lays ceramic plates and linen napkins on a wooden table. Safe.
5. Medium shot. Leo sits at the table alone, drawing with crayons on paper, swinging his legs, humming. The camera stays on the child. Child focused.
6. Close-up. Leo holds up his drawing of a dog and laughs; Maya's hand ruffles his hair. Child focused.
7. Close-up. Back at the counter, Maya's knife slips while cutting an apple; she gasps and pulls her hand back, a small cut on her fingertip with a thin line of blood. Medical trauma (mild).
8. Medium shot. Maya presses a paper towel to her finger, opens a first-aid box and wraps a plaster around it, wincing. Medical trauma (mild).
9. Wide shot. Maya, finger bandaged, pours coffee into two stoneware mugs and sits down at the table by the window, relaxed. Safe.

## Clip 2: The new sofa (dialogue clip)

**Style line:** Naturalistic indoor drama, 16:9, warm evening lamp light, static and slow-push camera, realistic people, a cozy living room with a large window, wooden floor and a few cardboard boxes.

**Cast:** SAM, a man in his early 40s with a short beard, wearing a navy flannel shirt. NINA, a woman in her late 30s with a straight black bob, wearing a mustard cardigan.

1. Wide shot. Sam and Nina stand in the half-furnished living room holding mugs. Nina: "I think the sofa goes under the window." Sam: "Then the lamp has nowhere to go." Safe.
2. Medium shot. Nina unrolls a large woven wool rug across the floor. Nina: "See? It already feels warmer in here." Sam: "It does. I take it back." Safe.
3. Medium shot. Sam assembles a tall brass floor lamp and switches it on; warm light fills the corner. Sam: "Better?" Nina, smiling: "Much better." Safe.
4. Two shot. They sit on a grey linen three-seat sofa with throw pillows, looking at a laptop. Nina: "This side table is nice, and it is on sale." Sam: "Add it to the list." Safe.
5. Close-up of Sam looking at a phone, his face tightening. Sam: "Wait. Did you move the money from the savings account?" Nina: "Only for the sofa. I told you last week." Tense, calm voices. Intense dialogue (rising).
6. Two shot, standing now. Sam, raising his voice: "You did not tell me! That was for the car!" Nina, louder: "You never listen to anything I say!" Both gesture sharply. Intense dialogue.
7. Close-up on Nina, angry and close to tears. Nina, shouting: "Fine! Send the sofa back then!" She throws a cushion onto the floor and walks out of frame. Intense dialogue.
8. Wide shot. Sam alone, sitting on the edge of the sofa with his head in his hands; silence, rain on the window. Somber.
9. Medium shot, later. Nina comes back with two cups of tea and sits beside him. Nina, softly: "I am sorry I yelled." Sam: "Me too. Let us keep the sofa." They both laugh quietly. Safe.

## Clip 3: Moving in

**Style line:** Soft cinematic short film, 16:9, overcast afternoon light turning to warm evening light, gentle dolly moves, realistic people, a small apartment with white walls, open cardboard boxes and a bedroom with a wooden bed frame.

**Cast:** ELLA, a woman in her late 20s with long red hair, wearing a cream oversized sweatshirt. JONAH, a man in his early 30s with short dark hair and glasses, wearing a green t-shirt.

1. Wide shot. Ella and Jonah carry boxes into the apartment and set them down by the door, next to a pile of sneakers and a canvas tote bag. Safe.
2. Medium shot. Ella unpacks a box and places a ceramic table lamp and a small potted plant on a wooden shelf. Safe.
3. Medium shot. Jonah unrolls a cotton duvet and pillows onto the bed, then smooths a knitted throw blanket over the end. Safe.
4. Close-up. Ella lifts a framed photo of an elderly woman out of a box. She stops, her eyes fill with tears. Death or grief.
5. Medium shot, muted colors. Short memory: a quiet funeral under grey sky, a closed wooden casket with white lilies, Ella in a black coat holding the same framed photo. Death or grief.
6. Two shot, back in the apartment. Jonah sits beside Ella on the floor and holds her while she cries quietly. Somber.
7. Medium shot, evening. Ella and Jonah, calmer, hang the framed photo on the wall and step back to look at it. Safe.
8. Close-up, low warm light. On the bed, Ella and Jonah kiss passionately and lie back; the camera tilts away to the window as the scene fades. Fully clothed, no nudity. Sexual (mild, implied).
9. Wide shot, next morning. Sunlight on the tidy bedroom; Jonah carries two mugs of coffee in. Safe.

## Clip 4: Errand

**Style line:** Grounded urban short film, 16:9, clear late afternoon light, handheld camera, realistic people, a bright home office at first, then a quiet city street with parked bicycles and shopfronts without signs.

**Cast:** OMAR, a man in his late 20s with short black hair and a light stubble, wearing a white t-shirt and an olive bomber jacket. A STRANGER in a dark hoodie, face partly hidden.

1. Medium shot. Omar works at a white standing desk with a laptop, an ergonomic mesh office chair and over-ear headphones; a desk lamp and a plant beside him. Safe.
2. Close-up. Omar stretches, takes off the headphones and sets them on a stand, glances at the time. Safe.
3. Medium shot. In the hallway Omar laces up white running sneakers, puts on the jacket and slings a black leather crossbody bag over his shoulder. Safe.
4. Wide shot. Omar walks down the quiet street, sipping from a steel water bottle. Safe.
5. Medium shot. The stranger runs up behind Omar, grabs the bag strap and shoves him hard; Omar stumbles against a wall. Violence.
6. Close-up. Omar and the stranger struggle over the bag; the stranger punches Omar in the shoulder, pulls the bag free and runs off. No blood. Violence.
7. Medium shot. Omar sits on the curb, breathing hard, a scrape on his palm; a passer-by kneels and asks if he is okay. Tense.
8. Medium shot, back home that evening. Omar, calmer, makes tea with a glass teapot and sits on a sofa under a blanket, phone in hand. Safe.

## After generating

1. Join each clip's shots in order (any editor, or `ffmpeg -f concat -safe 0 -i list.txt -c:v libx264 -crf 26 -preset slow -c:a aac -b:a 96k clipN.mp4`) and keep each file under 25 MB.
2. Put the 4 files in `data/samples/` and note the tool's name, so its terms can go in `docs/data-sources.md`.
3. The rest follows "When the clips arrive" in the resume notes.
