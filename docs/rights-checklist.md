# Rights checklist (one page)

Purpose: short list of rights actions for launch. General information, not legal advice.
Audience: PM.
Status: draft. Last updated: 2026-11-22.

## Sample clips
- [ ] Each sample is AI-generated by Sam, labeled "AI-generated" in the UI.
- [ ] Record for each: tool name, plan, date, and a link to the tool terms that allow commercial or public use. Terms for each tool: not yet checked.
- [ ] Manifest holds title, file, genre, duration, generator and attribution text.
- [ ] No commercial movie, TV or sports footage, frames, subtitles, scripts or posters anywhere.

## Catalog
- [ ] ABO attribution shown in the app and README: "Amazon.com" data, CC BY 4.0 link, citation of Collins et al., CVPR 2022.
- [ ] Product images shown are from ABO only.
- [ ] IAB taxonomy: file licence not confirmed. Ask IAB Tech Lab before committing the file.

## Uploads
- [ ] Show the notice before analysis: "Use only videos you own or are allowed to use. Pausall does not keep your video, frames, or audio after analysis. DRM-protected streaming services are not supported."
- [ ] Never store or log frames, audio or transcripts. Cache numeric results only, by content hash, short expiry.
- [ ] Feature flag allows launching with samples only.

## Simulated ads
- [ ] Label simulated ads, bids and numbers in the UI and docs.
