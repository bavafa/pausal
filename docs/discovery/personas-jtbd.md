# Personas and jobs to be done

Purpose: describe who Pausall is for and what each person is trying to get done.
Audience: reviewers, PM, design.
Status: draft. Last updated: 2026-11-22.

All content here is a hypothesis. No interviews have been run. Each persona becomes "validated" only after the interview plan in `interview-guide.md` is completed.

## 1. Streaming or retail media ad product owner (primary buyer persona)
- Job: "When I own the pause ad format, I want ads that fit the scene, so that viewers accept the format and advertisers see value."
- Pain (hypothesis): matching ads to a scene at the pause moment must be fast, safe and explainable.
- Success (hypothesis): a high share of pauses with a relevant ad, zero unsafe ads.

## 2. Brand advertiser or agency planner
- Job: "When I buy a pause placement, I want to know the scene is suitable and relevant, so that my brand is not shown in the wrong moment."
- Pain (hypothesis): little transparency on why an ad appeared.
- Success (hypothesis): clear suppression rules and a reason for each shown or withheld ad.

## 3. Viewer
- Job: "When I pause to take a break, I want nothing that feels like an interruption, and sometimes a product I actually care about."
- Pain (hypothesis): irrelevant or badly timed ads in the middle of a sad or tense scene.
- Success (hypothesis): quiet by default, relevant when shown, easy to dismiss.

## 4. Product leader reviewing this portfolio
- Job: "When I look at a candidate's work, I want to see sound judgement on scope, trade-offs, safety and measurement in a few minutes."
- Pain (hypothesis): demos without evidence, hidden failures, invented metrics.
- Success (hypothesis): a working demo in 15 seconds, a visible failure gallery and honest labels on everything simulated.
