## Leads worth chasing

- Read the primary GARM PDF on wfanet.org (URL surfaced in search but not read) to confirm the exact category list, tiers and 2022 update.
- Find what IAB Tech Lab "SCD flags" (sensitive content data) are; the taxonomy page links to them. Likely the closest current standard for sensitive-content signals.
- Read the EDPB Guidelines 2/2023 text (HTML or a text-extractable copy) for how contextual or server-side ad delivery is treated under Art. 5(3). Also read GDPR Recital 47 and ePrivacy Directive 2002/58 Art. 5(3) on EUR-Lex.
- Read the FTC Federal Register notice for exact COPPA effective and compliance dates; also check FTC guidance on contextual ads to children (the rule's definition of "support for internal operations" and contextual advertising carve-out).
- Check the MRC brand safety terminology notice (Oct 2025) at the MRC site, and the IAB Video Compliance Brief of December 2025 (https://www.iab.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/IAB_Video_Compliance_Brief_December_2025.pdf).
- Vendors not yet read: Wurl, IRIS.TV, Rembrand, Peacock In-Scene Ads, Zefr, IAS, DoubleVerify (video scene-level brand safety), Moments-level pause ad products on CTV platforms.
- Other US state laws (Virginia, Colorado, Texas and others) for targeted-advertising definitions; none read this run beyond California.

## Looked for and not found

- A current industry standard specifically for sensitive-content avoidance in video ads. Only the 2020 IAB brand safety guide, MRC measurement policy and the IAB taxonomy were surfaced; none read in full.
- A regulator statement (EDPB, CNIL, ICO) read this run that explicitly says contextual ads need no consent. The CNIL page and ICO page did not state this. The general view that contextual ads need less consent is therefore not confirmed by a primary source here.
- Primary GARM category text and an official statement of GARM successor or archive status.
- State privacy law text (other than the California AG page) on contextual advertising. The California page does not address contextual ads.
- Independent performance data (lift, accuracy) for any scene-level vendor. All claims are vendor-claim with no benchmarks.
- Brightcove, Mirriad and ISBA pages partly blocked (403) or lacked dates.
