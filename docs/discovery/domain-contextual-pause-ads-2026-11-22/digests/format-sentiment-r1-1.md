- The Amazon/Omnicom "The Power of the Pause" original (Adweek and Amazon Ads) to confirm the 183 sample and the contextual vs non-contextual comparison.
- eMarketer pieces on pause ads (VAB 51% action figure, Wunderkind/OpenGlass), which blocked fetching; try via another route.
- Statista "openness to pause ads by generation 2025" and its underlying survey publisher.
- Original Netflix Upfront materials (May 2025, Sept 2026) and the CES 2026 post for exact format wording.
- YouTube Help or Google Ads announcement for Pause ads, and any Disney Advertising or WBD pause-ad specs pages.
- Original Consumer Reports and YouGov surveys, to see whether any pause-specific question exists.
- IAB Tech Lab pause-ad standards or survey document (percentages, sample).

## Looked for and not found

- Any CPM, price or rate-card figure for pause ads at any platform.
- Any independent, non-seller measurement of pause ad performance (click-through rate, conversion).
- A published independent consumer survey that measures annoyance with pause ads specifically. Annoyance data found is about streaming ads in general, and pause-specific sentiment comes from industry-sponsored studies.
- A statement of targeting approach on Amazon's launch page.
- Verified launch details for Disney+ pause ads, Roku as a shipped product, and primary-source pages for YouTube and WBD.
- Fetch limits: eMarketer, Statista, The New Daily returned 403; Tom's Guide returned a truncated page. Budget was about 15 calls, so not every search result was opened.
