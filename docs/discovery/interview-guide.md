# Interview guide and survey (planned research)

Purpose: instruments for research that the PM will run personally.
Audience: PM.
Status: draft. Last updated: 2026-11-22. No interviews or surveys have been run. Do not cite any result from this document.

## Interview guide (30 minutes), ad product owners and advertisers
1. Tell me about the last time you decided where an ad should appear in video.
2. How do you decide a scene is unsuitable for an ad today?
3. What happens when an ad shows at a bad moment? Who finds out, and how?
4. How would you judge whether a pause ad was relevant? What would you measure?
5. What would make you trust an automated scene match? What would make you distrust it?
6. How important is an explanation for why an ad was shown or not shown?
7. What worries you most about contextual ads at a pause?
8. What would you need to see in a demo to believe it?
Close: who else should I talk to? May I contact you for follow-up?

## Viewer survey (5 minutes, planned)
1. How often do you pause streaming video? (Never, rarely, sometimes, often)
2. Have you seen an ad when you paused? (Yes, no, not sure)
3. How did you feel about it? (1 to 5, very negative to very positive)
4. If an ad appears at a pause, how relevant should it be to the scene? (open text)
5. In which scenes would an ad feel wrong? (select all: sad, scary, violent, romantic, children's, other)
6. Would you prefer that no ad appears when a scene is sensitive? (Yes, no, not sure)
Notes: collect no personal data beyond optional age band. State purpose and that responses are anonymous.
